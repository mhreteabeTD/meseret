# Stage 1 · Extract (content session)

Input: the textbook pages for ONE unit (PDF or photos). Output: `content/g{grade}/{subject}/u{unit}/inventory.md`. Stop after writing it; wait for approval.

List, in book order, every: unit title and outcomes; lesson/section number, title and pages; key-word box; start-up question; sub-heading; focus/fact box; table; figure (number + caption); activity and "Things to do" (with each question); exercise by its book name; unit summary; glossary; review exercise. Mark pages that seem missing. Then propose the idea split: one line per idea `idea-id · name · which section · mechanism|fact`. Nothing in this inventory may be dropped later.
